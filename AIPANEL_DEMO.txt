═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 KEYBOARD SHORTCUTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

GLOBAL (suggested implementation):
  Ctrl+A          Toggle AI panel focus
  Ctrl+\          Toggle AI panel collapse/expand

WHEN AI PANEL IS FOCUSED:
  Enter           Submit query to AI
  Esc             Unfocus AI panel (return to main content)
  Ctrl+Up         Increase panel height (+1 line, max 15)
  Ctrl+Down       Decrease panel height (-1 line, min 3)
  Any text        Type query in input field
